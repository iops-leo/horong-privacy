🏮 Horong — Privacy Policy

Language 한국어 · English

Effective 13 August 2026 · Version 1.4 (first effective 2 July 2026)

In short: Horong does not collect personally identifying information such as your name or email address. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your own device. For the purpose of improving the app, anonymous usage statistics (Firebase Analytics) are collected, and when you choose to watch a rewarded ad (Google AdMob), non-identifying information such as an advertising ID is processed. If the app crashes, a crash report (Firebase Crashlytics) is sent so we can find the cause.

1. Personal information we collect

Horong does not directly collect personally identifying information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. There is no sign-up and no login. For non-identifying information processed by third-party services (advertising and analytics), see section 3.

2. Data stored on your device

The following data is stored only inside your own device so that the game can work.

  • Puzzle progress (Journey levels, stars, daily records, streaks)
  • Which decorations you own and which are equipped
  • App settings (sound, reduce motion, gentle touch, showing time on the share card)
  • Anonymous usage statistics (dates the app was opened, completion counts and similar numeric data — no personally identifying information)

This data is deleted together with the app when you uninstall it.

3. Third parties and external services

Horong uses Google Firebase Analytics to improve app stability and features. The information sent consists solely of anonymized usage events (for example: app opened, tutorial completed, number of daily puzzles completed) and never includes personally identifying information such as a name, email address, or contact details. For how Firebase processes data, see the Firebase privacy documentation.

Horong uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to find the cause when the app shuts down unexpectedly. The information sent is limited to the technical detail needed to reproduce the problem — where the error occurred (a stack trace), the device model, the operating system version, and the app version. It never includes personally identifying information such as a name, email address, or contact details, and we use it only to fix the app, never for advertising.

Horong uses Google AdMob to provide rewarded ads. Ads are never shown to you unprompted — they appear only when you yourself choose "watch an ad for a reward". To serve ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may process an advertising ID, device information, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. Purchasing "Support · Remove ads" inside the app removes the ad feature. For details, see the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Advertising Technologies page.

In-app purchases (support and decorative items) are handled by the Google Play billing system. Horong has no access to your payment details, such as card numbers.

We do not send data to any other third-party service, including social logins. The "share" feature simply hands text to an app you choose yourself (a messenger, for example); Horong does not collect what you share.

4. For users in the EEA and the United Kingdom

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the controller for the limited processing described above is the developer of Horong, reachable at the address in section 7.

  • Legal basis. Analytics and advertising are carried out on the basis of your consent, requested through Google's consent form the first time the app runs.
  • Withdrawing consent. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings › Ad privacy settings. If you decline, the advertising SDK is not initialised at all.
  • Your rights. You have the right to access, rectify, erase, and port your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority. Because Horong holds no account and stores your progress only on your device, uninstalling the app removes everything we could hold about you; for anything processed by Google, please use the links in section 3.
  • Retention. We do not operate a server that stores your data. Retention of anonymous analytics and advertising data is governed by Google's own policies.

5. Future changes

If a new external service is introduced, or the way data is processed changes, we will update this policy first and give notice inside the app. Even then, our principle remains that no personally identifying information is collected.

6. Children's privacy

Horong is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not collect personal information from children either.

7. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact us by email.
